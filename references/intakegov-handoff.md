# IntakeGov Handoff

SPARI normally starts at `RECON_READY`.

This means the problem domain is clear enough for meaningful GitHub/PyPI reconnaissance even though full requirements may not yet exist.

Preferred handoff:

- raw intent;
- project relation;
- existing project context;
- known non-goals;
- known constraints;
- initial success criteria;
- technical environment;
- material unknowns.

After Broad Recon, SPARI returns a solution-space map and a few qualified questions. Confirmed intent then becomes the Golden Plan for Deep Research.
